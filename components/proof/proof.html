EVIDENCE

A Diagnosis Is Only Useful If You Can Explain It.

I don't want you to trust a clever explanation. I want the problem to be visible, understandable, and connected to something happening in the customer journey.

HOW I VALIDATE A FINDING

Every leak needs something behind it.

A page looking “bad” is not evidence. A personal preference is not evidence. Saying “customers don't like this” without knowing why is not evidence either.

I look for observable problems, behavioral signals, broken transitions, unclear decisions, and other evidence that explains where the journey starts losing momentum.
