THE HUNT

I Follow The Journey Until Something Breaks.

I don't start by changing things. I start by understanding what is happening, where the customer gets stuck, and what is actually causing the loss.

NO GUESSWORK

The goal isn't to find something to blame.

A weak conversion rate is a symptom. The real problem can be buried inside the message, the experience, the offer, the funnel, or the transition between them.

So I work backwards through the journey until the point of friction becomes clear.
