THE HUNT

I Follow The Journey Until Something Breaks.

I don't start by changing things just because they look wrong. First, I trace what the customer sees, what they do, and where that journey starts to break. Then I work backward to understand why.

01
TRACE 01

Map the Journey

Before looking for a problem, I need to see the path. Where did they come from? What did they see next? What were they asked to do? I map the journey from the first interaction to the point where action is expected.

HUNT 02

Find Where The Journey Slows

Then I look for the moments where behavior changes. Maybe people stop scrolling, abandon a form, ignore a CTA, or leave after reaching a certain step. Those moments tell us where to look closer.

UX Messaging CTAs
03
EXPOSE 03

Separate The Symptom From The Cause

A drop-off tells you that something happened. It does not tell you why. I look past the visible symptom to find what is actually getting in the way, whether that is confusion, missing trust, unnecessary friction, or a broken transition.

04
PRIORITIZE 04

Decide What Deserves Attention

Finding problems is not the same as knowing what to fix first. I separate the issues that matter from the ones that are merely noticeable, then focus on the points where a change can improve the journey most.

Impact Urgency Opportunity
05
FIX 05

Fix What Is Getting In The Way

Once the problem is clear, the investigation becomes action. That might mean rewriting the message, removing a step, strengthening the proof, fixing a transition, or changing what the customer sees next.
