WHAT I HUNT

Customers Rarely Tell You Where The Leak Is.

You can see that people are leaving. What you usually cannot see is why. A pause, a missed step, an abandoned form, or a sudden drop in engagement can all point to something happening beneath the surface. I look at those signals to find where the journey starts breaking down.

01 TRUST
◉

Trust Leaks

They understand what you offer, but they are not comfortable moving forward. The proof may be too weak, an important concern may be unanswered, or the page simply does not give them enough reason to believe you.

Weak proof Unanswered concerns Credibility gaps
02 CLARITY
Aa

Messaging Leaks

They should not have to work this hard to understand you. If they cannot quickly see what you offer, who it is for, or why it matters to them, interest can disappear before they ever take the next step.

Unclear value Weak positioning Confusing copy
03 FRICTION
↯

Friction Leaks

They are willing to continue, but something makes the next step harder than it should be. Too many clicks, unclear instructions, unnecessary choices, or a clumsy experience can turn interest into exit.

Too many steps Confusing UX Unnecessary effort
04 FLOW
↓

Funnel Leaks

Getting someone into the funnel is only the beginning. If each step fails to answer the question created by the step before it, people lose their reason to keep going and the drop-off starts to make sense.

Drop-off points Broken flow Weak CTAs
05 JOURNEY
↗

Customer Journey Leaks

A page can work perfectly well on its own and still create a problem when it follows an ad, email, or sales conversation that promised something different. The break often happens between touchpoints, not inside one.

Broken expectations Disconnected touchpoints Inconsistent experience
06 DECISION
?

Decision Leaks

Sometimes they have enough interest to keep going, but not enough certainty to act. An unanswered objection, an unclear next step, or one final piece of missing information can leave the decision hanging.

Hesitation Unclear next step Unresolved objections
THE INVESTIGATION

Don't just ask: "Why did they leave?"

Ask: "Where did the journey stop making sense?"
