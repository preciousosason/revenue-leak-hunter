WHAT I HUNT

Customers Rarely Tell You Where The Leak Is.

They leave clues instead. A hesitation. A question that isn't answered. A page they never finish. A form they abandon. A purchase they almost made. The leak is usually hidden inside the journey.

THE CLUES ARE DIFFERENT

Not every lost customer leaves for the same reason.

Sometimes they don't trust you enough. Sometimes they don't understand the offer. Sometimes the process asks too much from them.

And sometimes everything looks fine on its own, but the pieces don't connect when the customer moves from one touchpoint to the next.
