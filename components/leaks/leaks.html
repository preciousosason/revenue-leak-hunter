WHAT I HUNT

Customers Rarely Tell You Where The Leak Is.

Customers rarely tell you exactly why they stopped. They leave signals instead. Hesitation, confusion, drop-off, abandonment, or a broken transition. I look for what those signals reveal.

01 TRUST
◉

Trust Leaks

They are interested, but something makes them hesitate. They aren't sure you are credible, safe, experienced, or worth the risk.

Weak proof Missing reassurance Credibility gaps
02 CLARITY
Aa

Messaging Leaks

They cannot quickly understand what you offer, who it is for, why it matters, or what makes taking the next step worthwhile.

Unclear value Weak positioning Confusing copy
03 FRICTION
↯

Friction Leaks

They want to continue, but the experience makes moving forward harder than it needs to be.

Too many steps Confusing UX Decision fatigue
04 FLOW
↓

Funnel Leaks

People enter with interest, but the funnel fails to give them the right information, motivation, or next step at the right moment.

Drop-off points Broken flow Weak CTAs
05 JOURNEY
↗

Customer Journey Leaks

Each part of the experience may look fine alone, but the transition between ads, pages, emails, sales, and checkout breaks the customer's momentum.

Broken expectations Disconnected touchpoints Inconsistent experience
06 DECISION
?

Decision Leaks

They are interested enough to continue, but something prevents them from making the decision. Uncertainty, hesitation, competing priorities, or a weak next step can quietly stop momentum.

Decision hesitation Weak next steps Unresolved objections
THE INVESTIGATION

Don't just ask: "Why did they leave?"

Ask: "Where did the journey stop making sense?"
