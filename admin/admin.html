Metric definitions & data coverage

Reports use sessions that started in the selected range, with activity and verified enquiries up to the range end. Conversion rate = sessions with a verified enquiry ÷ sessions. Engagement = at least 10 seconds of tracked foreground activity, two page views, or a verified enquiry. Visitors are browser identifiers, not identified people. Comparisons use the immediately preceding interval of equal elapsed length; today's data is partial.

Form completion uses matching form-attempt IDs and event order. Lead stages show current status, not historical stage snapshots. Existing v1 data is preserved in its original tables and is excluded because its conversion and engagement definitions were unreliable. Tracking can be absent because of consent choices, blockers, offline activity, or installation gaps. Attribution is browser supplied; the enquiry itself is server confirmed.
