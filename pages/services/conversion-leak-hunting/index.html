THE LEAK

TRAFFIC ≠ CONVERSION

Getting people there is only half the job.

Someone can click your ad, find you through search, land on your website, understand roughly what you sell and still leave without doing anything.

That does not automatically mean you need more traffic.

The leak may already exist inside the experience people are being sent to.

THE QUESTION Where does interest stop becoming forward movement?
02

THE BREAKDOWN

MOMENTUM DOESN'T DISAPPEAR MAGICALLY

Somewhere between attention and action, something changes.

01

They don't understand.

The offer, value, audience, outcome, or next step is not clear enough quickly enough.

CLARITY LEAK
02

They don't see enough value.

The experience explains what exists without making the reason to care sufficiently clear.

VALUE LEAK
03

They don't trust it yet.

Questions, objections, proof, risk, credibility, or reassurance have not been resolved at the decision point.

TRUST LEAK
04

Acting feels harder than leaving.

Weak calls-to-action, unnecessary steps, forms, choices, or confusing paths create resistance at the worst possible moment.

THE INVESTIGATION

FOLLOW THE LEAK

I investigate the journey, not just the conversion rate.

A conversion rate tells you that something happened. It does not necessarily tell you why. The investigation looks underneath the number at the experience producing it.

01
VALUE CLARITY

Can people understand why this matters to them?

I examine positioning, headline clarity, offer communication, relevance and whether the page answers the visitor's most basic question: why should I care?

02
DECISION PATH

Does the experience naturally move people forward?

I look at how information, benefits, proof, objections, choices and calls-to-action work together instead of behaving like unrelated pieces of a page.

03
TRUST

What doubt remains when the visitor is asked to commit?

I examine credibility signals, proof, reassurance, unanswered questions and the points where the experience asks for more trust than it has earned.

04
ACTION FRICTION

What gets in the way when someone is ready?

I inspect calls-to-action, forms, unnecessary steps, competing choices and other obstacles that can turn intent into abandonment.

NO FAIRY TALES

CONVERSION IS NOT MIND CONTROL

The goal isn't to make every visitor buy.

Some visitors are not ready. Some are not qualified. Some will compare alternatives. Some simply do not want what you sell.

Good conversion work does not manipulate every human who touches the page into surrendering their credit card.

It removes unnecessary reasons for the right visitor to become confused, doubtful, frustrated, or lost.

THE OBJECTIVE Make the path clearer. Make the value easier to understand. Make the decision easier to evaluate. Remove avoidable friction.
