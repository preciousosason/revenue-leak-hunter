01 / THE LEAK

More traffic cannot fix a broken journey.

Businesses often react to weak conversions by trying to attract more visitors.

But if customers are already arriving and something inside the experience is pushing them away, more traffic simply sends more people into the same leak.

The useful question is not only “How do we get more people here?”

It is also: “What happens after they arrive?”

03 / THE INVESTIGATION

I don't just look at pages. I look at movement.

The investigation focuses on the signals, decisions and friction that shape how customers move through the experience.

01

Entry-Point Alignment

Whether ads, search results, social content, referrals and landing pages create a consistent expectation.

02

Message Clarity

Whether customers can understand the offer without decoding vague positioning, jargon or unnecessary complexity.

03

Friction

Forms, navigation, checkout steps, confusing choices, unnecessary effort and other obstacles that interrupt action.

04

Trust Signals

Reviews, evidence, transparency, guarantees, credibility indicators and the questions customers need answered.

05

Decision Architecture

Calls to action, hierarchy, choices, objections and whether the experience naturally leads toward the next step.

06

Post-Conversion Experience

What customers experience after taking action, including confirmation, onboarding and opportunities for retention.

04 / THE METHOD

Follow the customer. Find the break.

01

02

03

04

Repair

Prioritize the changes most likely to remove friction and create a stronger path toward action.

06 / THE OBJECTIVE

Turn a fragmented journey into a deliberate one.

LEAKING JOURNEY
Discovery → Confusion → Doubt → Exit

Customers are forced to figure out what to do, why to trust you and whether continuing is worth the effort.

STRONGER JOURNEY
Discovery → Clarity → Trust → Action

Each stage answers the questions required to make the next decision easier, clearer and more natural.

07 / NO FAIRY TALES

Customer journey optimization is not mind control.

No serious investigation can promise that every visitor will convert.

Customers have different needs, budgets, priorities and reasons for leaving.

What we can do is remove unnecessary reasons for the right customer to disappear.
