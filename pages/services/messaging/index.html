THE MESSAGE HAS ONE JOB Reduce unnecessary uncertainty.
01
THE LEAK

Confusion creates friction before the customer ever reaches the button.

You know what you sell. You know how it works. You know why it is useful.

Your visitor does not have that advantage.

They arrive with questions. What is this? Is it for me? Why does it matter? Can I trust it? What should I do next?

If your message does not answer those questions clearly, the visitor has to start doing the interpretation.

THE PROBLEM

The harder someone has to work to understand your offer, the more opportunities you create for hesitation.

02

NO MAGIC WORDS

Clearer messaging does not hypnotize people into buying.

Copy cannot rescue a bad product, manufacture demand that does not exist, or force the wrong customer to convert.

What stronger messaging can do is remove unnecessary confusion, make value easier to evaluate, support important claims, and give qualified customers a clearer path toward a decision.

MESSAGE LEAK INVESTIGATION

If customers have to decode your offer before they can value it, the message is already creating friction.

Find the confusion before spending more money sending people into it.
