01 / THE BLIND SPOT

Two numbers can hide an entire customer journey.

You know how many people arrived.

You know how many converted.

But between those two numbers, people clicked, hesitated, explored, abandoned forms, returned, ignored calls-to-action, moved between pages, or disappeared completely.

That behavior is where many useful clues live.

If those actions are not measured properly, the final conversion rate tells you that something happened without showing you where to investigate next.

07 / NO DATA THEATER

Analytics can show behavior. It cannot read minds.

A drop-off tells us where something happened, not automatically why a person made the decision they made.

Good conversion intelligence narrows the search. It gives us evidence, reveals patterns, challenges assumptions, and shows where deeper investigation or testing makes sense.

The goal is not pretending data knows everything. The goal is making fewer decisions in the dark.
