01
THE LEAK

A funnel is a journey, not a pile of marketing assets.

Someone sees an ad. They click. They reach a landing page. They become interested. They submit a form.

Then the experience changes.

The follow-up takes too long. The message no longer matches the promise. The next step is unclear. Or nothing useful happens at all.

Every individual component may technically be working while the overall journey is quietly losing momentum.

CORE PRINCIPLE Every stage should prepare the customer for the next decision.

Funnel architecture examines those transitions as one connected system instead of judging each page, form, or email in isolation.

CONNECTED FUNNEL

07 / NO FAIRY TALES

Better funnel architecture does not hypnotize people into buying.

A connected funnel cannot make a weak offer desirable or guarantee that every visitor becomes a customer.

The job is simpler and more useful: remove unnecessary breaks in the journey so interested people can understand what happens next and continue without avoidable confusion or friction.

Good architecture does not force momentum. It stops wasting it.
