My Philosophy

Scaling a Broken System Only Makes the Problem Bigger.

If a business is already losing customers somewhere in its journey, sending more traffic into that system doesn't automatically solve anything.

It can simply create more opportunities for the same leak to cost more money.

Find the problem first. Understand why it exists. Fix what matters. Then scale.
