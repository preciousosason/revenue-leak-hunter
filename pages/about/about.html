The Perspective

More attention does not fix a broken journey.

Getting someone through the door is only the beginning.

A visitor still has to understand what they are looking at, recognise the value, trust the offer, know what to do next, and move through the experience without unnecessary friction.

When one of those steps breaks, the problem is often difficult to see from the inside of the business.

That is where I work.

ATTENTION → CLARITY → TRUST → ACTION

My Philosophy

Don't scale what you haven't understood.

More traffic can amplify a good system. It can also amplify a bad one.

Before adding more campaigns, more content, or more traffic, I want to understand what happens after someone arrives.

Find the friction. Understand the cause. Fix what matters. Then build from there.
