Before You Read

About this portfolio

These articles were created specifically as professional writing samples.

They were independently researched and written by Precious Elijah to demonstrate my ability to research unfamiliar subjects, work with credible information, structure long-form content, and explain health and wellness topics clearly to everyday readers.

They are portfolio samples, not commissioned client work, and are hosted on Leakendia solely for professional review. Health and wellness is not Leakendia's usual editorial or service focus, and these articles should not be interpreted as representing the normal subject matter of the Leakendia brand.

The articles are educational in nature. They do not provide individualized medical, nutritional, fitness, or healthcare advice. Health claims should be understood in the context of the reputable guidance and research used to support each piece, while personal medical concerns belong with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
