A quick note

I haven't worked professionally in health and wellness yet.

So instead of pretending otherwise, I made these samples.

Each article started with a question. I researched it, compared information from reputable sources, worked out what actually mattered to the reader, and then wrote the article around that.

That's really what I wanted this page to show: how I think, research, structure information, and write when I'm dealing with a subject where getting the details right matters.

The articles are educational writing samples, not personal medical, nutritional, or fitness advice.

Research

Start with reliable information, not whatever sounds good.

Understand

Work out what the information actually means before trying to explain it.

Write

Make it useful to the person reading it.
